**Thread and seat.** Entry requires actual tip in the tapped mouth, radial error <3 mm and axis error <10°. A swept 1 ms crossing detects the phase-correct catch plane, avoiding a missed catch during gravity settling. It never corrects the pin in the clear ear. Once caught, an ideal screw manifold projects axial/angular momentum at **2.5 mm/rev**, clockwise from head advances down. Shared visual phase is `z_tip = z_seat + pitch * spin_about_+Z/(2π)` modulo pitch; measured pre-seat residual ≤**0.000 μm**. Only the first catch can apply a substep axial correction (recorded in the thread event); no arbitrary task-stage snapping. Assumed passive drag is the flange Astra harness's 0.020 Nm breakaway / 0.015 Nm sliding / 0.002 Nm·s/rad damping. Pulling beyond entry +3 mm or unscrewing beyond −0.1 turn releases. Collar arrival activates a finite 3 ms world-to-pin seat weld at the matching zero-spin phase; reverse deflection >0.5° releases that stop. Success requires the seat constraint latched and collar gap <0.5 mm. This is a feasibility surrogate, not calibrated thread friction, cross-threading, tightening torque or adhesive failure physics.
